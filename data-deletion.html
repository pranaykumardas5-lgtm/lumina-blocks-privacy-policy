Data Deletion Instructions

How to request deletion of Lumina Blocks account data

Request account-data deletion

To request deletion of information associated with your Lumina Blocks account, email arjunkumar1233i49448@gmail.com with the subject “Lumina Blocks Data Deletion Request”.

Please include:

  • Your in-game Player ID.
  • The sign-in method you used (Google, Facebook, or Guest).
  • An email address we can use to respond, if applicable.

Do not send passwords, one-time codes, access tokens, or your Facebook/Google login credentials. We may request reasonable information to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

What happens next?

We will review the request and delete eligible account information under our control, subject to applicable legal, security, and technical requirements. Information held independently by Google, Meta/Facebook, or another provider may need to be managed through that provider directly.

Deleting the app or clearing its storage may remove local Guest progress, but does not necessarily delete data linked to an online account.
